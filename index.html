What is Rail Fence Cipher?

Rail Fence is a transposition cipher. It does not change the actual letters. Instead, it rearranges their positions by writing them in a zig-zag pattern across multiple rails. First, write characters in zig-zag order. To encrypt, read characters row by row. To decrypt, reconstruct the zig-zag pattern and read along its path.
